The Linear Programming Formulation

Problem Formulation Overview Prime-broker custody allocation is structured as a continuous constrained linear program (LP). The objective is to determine the optimal allocation vector ‭\(\mathbf{x}\)‬ that minimizes the fund’s total weekly financing and borrow expense while strictly satisfying contractual covenants, borrow access rules, concentration thresholds, and regulatory margin requirements. Decision Variables & Objective Function Let the primary decision variable be: ‭\[x_{i,p} \in [0, 1] \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; p \in \{1,\dots,P\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ Where ‭\(x_{i,p}\)‬ denotes the continuous proportion of position ‭\(i\)‬ allocated to prime broker ‭\(p\)‬. The Objective Function The objective minimizes total annualized financing expense expressed on a weekly compounding basis: ‭\[\min_{\mathbf{x}, s_{\text{pm}}} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{p=1}^P \left( x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \cdot r_{i,p} \cdot \frac{1}{52} \right) + c_{\text{slack}} \cdot s_{\text{pm}}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ Where: ⚬ ‭\(N_i\)‬: The gross market value (notional USD) of position ‭\(i\)‬. ⚬ ‭\(r_{i,p}\)‬: The annualized net financing or borrow rate charged on position ‭\(i\)‬ by prime broker ‭\(p\)‬ (comprising the long debit spread, short rebate spread, or hard-to-borrow locate surcharge). ⚬ ‭\(s_{\text{pm}}\)‬: Epigraph slack variable representing Portfolio-Margin risk requirement at PB-B. ⚬ ‭\(c_{\text{slack}}\)‬: Marginal cost weight applied to portfolio-margin capital lockup (set to 0 for pure cash-drag minimization). Operational & Regulatory Constraints The optimization space is defined by six distinct constraint classes: 1. Full Custody Assignment Every position must be 100% custodied across the network; no short or long exposure may remain unallocated: ‭\[\sum_{p=1}^P x_{i,p} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ 2. Hard-to-Borrow (HTB) Availability If position \(i\) is designated as hard-to-borrow (\(\text{is\_htb}_i = \text{TRUE}\)) and prime broker \(p\) does not possess borrow inventory (\(\text{htb\_avail}_p = \text{FALSE}\)), the allocation is forced to zero: \[x_{i,p} = 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\} \quad \text{if } \text{is\_htb}_i \land \neg\,\text{htb\_avail}_p\]

  1. Contractual Balance Floor Covenants To preserve active credit facilities, access trading desks, and prevent account fee surcharges, each prime broker enforces a minimum gross notional custody floor ‭\(\underline{B}_p\)‬: ‭\[\sum_{i=1}^N x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \ge \underline{B}_p \quad \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
  2. Single-Name Position Concentration Caps To mitigate idiosyncratic counterparty and liquidity risk, each broker caps the total exposure of any individual position to a contractual fraction ‭\(\kappa_p\)‬ of total Fund NAV: ‭\[x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \le \kappa_p \cdot \text{NAV} \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
  3. Aggregate Issuer Concentration Caps For companies with multiple share classes, debt, or paired issues (‭\(\mathcal{I}_k\)‬), total firm-level exposure allocated to prime broker ‭\(p\)‬ cannot breach the issuer threshold ‭\(\kappa^{\text{iss}}_p\)‬: ‭\[\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_k} x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \le \kappa^{\text{iss}}_p \cdot \text{NAV} \quad \forall k, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
  4. Portfolio Margin Beta-Grid Regime (PB-B) PB-B calculates margin requirements using a risk-based portfolio margin grid. The requirement evaluates net portfolio loss across a 10-point systemic market stress grid ‭\(\delta_k \in [-15\%, +15\%]\)‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬: ‭\[s_{\text{pm}} \ge \sum_{i=1}^N x_{i,\text{PB\_B}} \cdot \left( -\delta_k \cdot \beta_i \cdot \text{side}_i \cdot N_i \right) \quad \forall k \in \{1,\dots,10\}\]‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ Where ‭\(\text{side}_i = +1\)‬‭‬‭‬ for long positions and ‭\(-1\)‬ for short positions. The epigraph formulation converts the non-linear ‭\(\max_k(\dots)\)‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ risk envelope into 10 smooth linear inequalities. Continuous LP vs. Discrete MILP: Dual Shadow Pricing

The primary framework utilizes a continuous relaxation (‭\(x_{i,p} \in [0, 1]\)‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬) rather than restricting allocations to binary integers (‭\(x_{i,p} \in \{0, 1\}\)‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬).

This formulation provides two distinct mathematical and operational advantages:

  1. Dual Shadow Pricing (‭\(\lambda\)‬): Continuous formulations provide exact Lagrange multipliers (dual values) for each constraint. This tells the CFO precisely how many dollars per year an extra $1M of covenant flexibility or a 1% shift in single-name concentration cap would save the fund.
  2. Convex Geometry: Continuous LPs scale efficiently via the Simplex or Interior Point algorithms, guaranteeing global optimality in milliseconds without the exponential combinatorial search of integer programming.

Note: For the operational evaluation where custody splitting is prohibited (‭\(x_{i,p} \in \{0, 1\}\)‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬), refer to Chapter 7: Appendix (MILP & Technical Specs).

Problem Formulation Overview
Prime-broker custody allocation is structured as a continuous constrained linear program (LP). The objective is to determine the optimal allocation vector ‭$\mathbf{x}$‬ that minimizes the fund's total weekly financing and borrow expense while strictly satisfying contractual covenants, borrow access rules, concentration thresholds, and regulatory margin requirements.
Decision Variables & Objective Function
Let the primary decision variable be:
‭$$x_{i,p} \in [0, 1] \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; p \in \{1,\dots,P\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
Where ‭$x_{i,p}$‬ denotes the continuous proportion of position ‭$i$‬ allocated to prime broker ‭$p$‬.
The Objective Function
The objective minimizes total annualized financing expense expressed on a weekly compounding basis:
‭$$\min_{\mathbf{x}, s_{\text{pm}}} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{p=1}^P \left( x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \cdot r_{i,p} \cdot \frac{1}{52} \right) + c_{\text{slack}} \cdot s_{\text{pm}}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
Where:
⚬   ‭$N_i$‬: The gross market value (notional USD) of position ‭$i$‬.
⚬   ‭$r_{i,p}$‬: The annualized net financing or borrow rate charged on position ‭$i$‬ by prime broker ‭$p$‬ (comprising the long debit spread, short rebate spread, or hard-to-borrow locate surcharge).
⚬   ‭$s_{\text{pm}}$‬: Epigraph slack variable representing Portfolio-Margin risk requirement at PB-B.
⚬   ‭$c_{\text{slack}}$‬: Marginal cost weight applied to portfolio-margin capital lockup (set to 0 for pure cash-drag minimization).
Operational & Regulatory Constraints
The optimization space is defined by six distinct constraint classes:
1. Full Custody Assignment
Every position must be 100% custodied across the network; no short or long exposure may remain unallocated:
‭$$\sum_{p=1}^P x_{i,p} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
2. Hard-to-Borrow (HTB) Availability
   If position $i$ is designated as hard-to-borrow ($\text{is\_htb}_i = \text{TRUE}$) and prime broker $p$ does not possess borrow inventory ($\text{htb\_avail}_p = \text{FALSE}$), the allocation is forced to zero:
   $$x_{i,p} = 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\} \quad \text{if } \text{is\_htb}_i \land \neg\,\text{htb\_avail}_p$$
  

3. Contractual Balance Floor Covenants
To preserve active credit facilities, access trading desks, and prevent account fee surcharges, each prime broker enforces a minimum gross notional custody floor ‭$\underline{B}_p$‬:
‭$$\sum_{i=1}^N x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \ge \underline{B}_p \quad \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
4. Single-Name Position Concentration Caps
To mitigate idiosyncratic counterparty and liquidity risk, each broker caps the total exposure of any individual position to a contractual fraction ‭$\kappa_p$‬ of total Fund NAV:
‭$$x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \le \kappa_p \cdot \text{NAV} \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,N\}, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
5. Aggregate Issuer Concentration Caps
For companies with multiple share classes, debt, or paired issues (‭$\mathcal{I}_k$‬), total firm-level exposure allocated to prime broker ‭$p$‬ cannot breach the issuer threshold ‭$\kappa^{\text{iss}}_p$‬:
‭$$\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_k} x_{i,p} \cdot N_i \le \kappa^{\text{iss}}_p \cdot \text{NAV} \quad \forall k, \; \forall p \in \{1,\dots,P\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
6. Portfolio Margin Beta-Grid Regime (PB-B)
PB-B calculates margin requirements using a risk-based portfolio margin grid. The requirement evaluates net portfolio loss across a 10-point systemic market stress grid ‭$\delta_k \in [-15\%, +15\%]$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬‭‬:
‭$$s_{\text{pm}} \ge \sum_{i=1}^N x_{i,\text{PB\_B}} \cdot \left( -\delta_k \cdot \beta_i \cdot \text{side}_i \cdot N_i \right) \quad \forall k \in \{1,\dots,10\}$$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬
Where ‭$\text{side}_i = +1$‬‭‬‭‬ for long positions and ‭$-1$‬ for short positions. The epigraph formulation converts the non-linear ‭$\max_k(\dots)$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ risk envelope into 10 smooth linear inequalities.

### Continuous LP vs. Discrete MILP: Dual Shadow Pricing
The primary framework utilizes a continuous relaxation (‭$x_{i,p} \in [0, 1]$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬) rather than restricting allocations to binary integers (‭$x_{i,p} \in \{0, 1\}$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬).

This formulation provides two distinct mathematical and operational advantages: 

1. **Dual Shadow Pricing (‭$\lambda$‬):** Continuous formulations provide exact Lagrange multipliers (dual values) for each constraint. This tells the CFO precisely how many dollars per year an extra $1M of covenant flexibility or a 1% shift in single-name concentration cap would save the fund.
2. **Convex Geometry:** Continuous LPs scale efficiently via the Simplex or Interior Point algorithms, guaranteeing global optimality in milliseconds without the exponential combinatorial search of integer programming.
    
*Note: For the operational evaluation where custody splitting is prohibited (‭$x_{i,p} \in \{0, 1\}$‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ ‭‬‭‬), refer to [Chapter 7: Appendix (MILP & Technical Specs)](07-appendix.qmd).*

Author

Published

September 18, 2026

Introduction

On a $400M gross / $60M net long/short book, an unconstrained naive financing allocation leaves a fund acutely exposed to margin calls during market dislocations. By formulating prime-broker custody as a continuous linear program (LP), we determine that the stress-robust allocation guaranteeing margin survival across 1987-, 2008-, and 2020-style factor shocks costs +$36,074 per year more than the naive minimum—a mere 0.9 bps of financing spread drag to eliminate liquidation vulnerability.
